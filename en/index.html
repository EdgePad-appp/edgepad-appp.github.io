Features

It kicks in only when you want it to

A gesture starts only when one finger touches the strip next to an edge and moves along it, or when you double-tap a top corner. Everything else works the way your trackpad always does.

Scrub music and videonew

Spotify, Music, QuickTime and VLC are scrubbed directly, even in the background, with the exact time on the timeline. Video in the browser is scrubbed with the ← → keys.

Tracks from the top cornersnew

Double-tap the left corner for the previous track and the right one for the next. You can change what each corner does.

Scroll soundnew

A soft click on every step. The higher the level, the higher the pitch. The click volume is adjustable.

Zones that fit your hand

Each edge can be 3 to 40% wide and each corner 8 to 30 mm. The settings show how big that is on your trackpad.

Everything else stays the same

Taps, clicks, scrolling and multi-finger gestures work as usual. A palm resting on the trackpad while you type is ignored.

A system-style indicator

The level, the track timeline or the action name at the bottom of the screen. The pointer stays put during a gesture.

Install

Four steps, once

  1. Drag it to Applications

    Open the downloaded EdgePad.dmg and drag EdgePad into the Applications folder.

  2. Allow the first launch

    macOS will warn that it can’t verify the developer. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and, under Security, allow EdgePad to open.

  3. Turn on Accessibility

    When EdgePad asks, turn it on in the Accessibility list. Without it, corners, scrubbing and the pointer lock don’t work.

  4. Let it control your players

    The first time you scrub Spotify or Music, macOS asks whether EdgePad may control them. Click Allow.

EdgePad isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so step 2 is needed on the first launch. The EdgePad icon then appears in the menu bar, and the settings window opens on its own.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does EdgePad cost?

The first 7 days are free, with every feature. After that it’s $2 once, with no subscription. One key works on three of your Macs, and 1.x updates are free.

Is the app in English?

Not yet: the EdgePad interface is in Ukrainian for now. The gestures work the same either way, and wherever this page mentions a button in the app, its Ukrainian label is shown next to it.

How do I activate my key?

The key arrives by email right after payment. Open EdgePad’s settings, go to the License tab («Ліцензія»), paste the key and click Activate («Активувати»). To move the key to another Mac, click Release key («Звільнити ключ») on the same tab.

What happens when the trial ends?

The trackpad edges and corners stop controlling your Mac until you enter a key. Your settings stay put, and the trackpad keeps working as usual.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked. Email us or reply to your receipt. Details are in the refund policy.

Why does macOS show a warning on first launch?

EdgePad isn’t signed and notarized by Apple yet, so macOS won’t open it right away. You allow it once in System Settings → Privacy & Security. After that it opens normally.

How does scrubbing work in Spotify and Music?

Directly: EdgePad moves the playback position itself, even when the app is in the background, and the indicator shows the title, the timeline and how many seconds you’ve moved. QuickTime Player and VLC work the same way. The step can be set from 2 to 30 seconds.

What about video in the browser?

Video in Safari, Chrome and other browsers, as well as in IINA and Apple TV, is scrubbed with the ← → keys, so the video window needs to be active. If music is playing at the same time, the music is scrubbed instead.

How do I switch tracks?

Double-tap or double-click the top-left corner of the trackpad for the previous track and the top-right corner for the next. A single tap in a corner works as a normal click, just a moment later.

Why does it need Accessibility permission?

To press the scrubbing and media keys for you and to keep the pointer in place during a gesture. Volume and brightness work without it.

Will it get in the way of scrolling and macOS gestures?

No. A gesture starts only when one finger touches a colored strip and moves along the edge. Two-finger scrolling, system gestures, taps and clicks work as usual. If a strip triggers by accident, make it narrower, down to 3%.

Can it change the brightness of an external display?

No, EdgePad controls the brightness of the MacBook’s built-in display. Regular external displays adjust brightness with their own buttons.

What are the system requirements?

A Mac with a trackpad and macOS 13 Ventura or later. The app is universal, for Macs with Apple Silicon and Intel.
